Kodachadri Summit Trail: Western Ghats Elevation Breakdown

Situated within the biodiverse belt of the Mookambika Wildlife Sanctuary, the Kodachadri ascent presents a continuous transition from tropical rainforest canopies to steep granite water gullies and wind-sculpted Shola ridgelines.

Peak Telemetry
1,343 m (4,406 ft)
Trek Distance
14.2 km Loop
Ascent Gradient
Moderate - Technical
Primary Waterway
Hidlumane Gorge

Critical Trail Sectors

  • Hidlumane Waterfall Corridor: A steep multi-level granite scramble over slick river boulders demanding deliberate foot placement.
  • Upper Ridge Transition: The emergence from dense forest into open, high-exposure Montane Shola grasslands.
  • Sarvajna Peetha Crest: The solitary summit monolith structure overlooking the Arabian Sea horizon.

Because entry permits are strictly regulated by the Karnataka Forest Department with mandatory checkpoint quotas, hikers looking for pre-secured forest permits, homestay accommodation, and round-trip transport from Bengaluru can check the complete Kodachadri trekking expedition schedule.
